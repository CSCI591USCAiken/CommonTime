Where do my answers go?

Directly to the organizer's Google Form. The form inside this page is the real form; this website does not keep a separate copy of your answers.

Does submitting book a meeting?

Submitting shares your availability. Your organizer will confirm the meeting details. Automated calendar scheduling depends on their CommonTime add-on setup.

What if the form doesn't open?

Try the “Open it in a new tab” link below the form. If Google says the form is not accepting responses, the organizer needs to publish or reopen it.
